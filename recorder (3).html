Tips for a clean recording
  • Quiet room, phone about 10–15 cm from the mouth, same distance every time.
  • Letter sounds: say the Amharic sound shown. The English line is the same sound in Latin letters (a capital letter means the heavy sound, for example Ta, Sa). “Letter name” rows are the name of the letter.
  • The orange note box appears for letters with a special sound (th, ḥ, dh). Read it before recording that section.
  • Words: read the Arabic word at teaching speed. The Amharic and English lines are the pronunciation guide; the grey line is the meaning, so do not say it. The green tags show which letter sound the word teaches (short, long, initial form…) and where it sits in the word. A red warning means your sheet marks the word as unconfirmed.
  • Many rows of one letter have the same sound (initial, medial, final). Record one, then tap Copy to same-sound rows to fill the empty ones.
  • Silence is trimmed and the volume is levelled automatically. Re-record any clip to replace it. Progress is saved on this device.
  • Keys: Space = record/stop, ← → = previous/next, P = play.
